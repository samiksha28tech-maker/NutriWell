Health and safety disclaimer

NutriWell is an educational nutrition and lifestyle tracking application. It is not a substitute for a qualified doctor, registered dietitian or other healthcare professional.

Meal suggestions, exercise information, hydration records and progress summaries are general information. They are not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent disease. The sample plans in this starter project have not been clinically validated for individual users.

If you have asthma or another lung condition, diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease, kidney disease, joint problems, take medication, or are unsure whether an activity is appropriate, consult a qualified healthcare professional before following a plan. Do not use the app to change medication or prescribed treatment.

Stop exercising if you experience chest pain, severe breathlessness, dizziness or other concerning symptoms and seek appropriate medical help.

NutriWell records should not be treated as medical records or emergency monitoring. Protect your login details and do not enter information you do not wish to store.
